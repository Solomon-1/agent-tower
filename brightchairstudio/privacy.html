BrightChair Pinner privacy policy

BrightChair Pinner is a private tool used only by the owner of the BrightChairStudio Etsy shop. It posts pins of the shop's own Etsy listings to the shop's own Pinterest account.

What it accesses

It reads the shop's own Pinterest boards and pins, and creates boards and pins on that one account. It does not access any other Pinterest user's data.

What it collects

Nothing from anyone else. It collects no personal information from visitors or buyers, uses no cookies or tracking, and never sells or shares data.

Storage and removal

The Pinterest access token is stored privately in the shop owner's Google account. Access can be revoked at any time in Pinterest under Settings, Security and logins, Apps.
